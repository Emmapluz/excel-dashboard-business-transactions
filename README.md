## Project Overview

An interactive Excel dashboard built on 
2,000 business transactions analysing 
revenue, expenses and profit across 
payment methods, product lines, regions, 
categories and departments. Built using 
5 pivot tables connected to 5 different 
chart types with a slicer for dynamic 
filtering.

---

## Dataset Columns

```
Transaction_ID, Transaction_Date,
Revenue, Expenses, Profit,
Category, Region, Department,
Product_Line, Customer_Segment,
Payment_Method

```

---

## Pivot Tables Built

| Pivot | Metric | Dimension |
|---|---|---|
| Pivot 1 | Sum of Profit | Payment Method |
| Pivot 2 | Sum of Revenue | Product Line |
| Pivot 3 | Revenue + Expenses + Profit | Category |
| Pivot 4 | Count of Transactions | Region |
| Pivot 5 | Average Expenses | Department |

---

## Charts Built

| Chart | Type | Insight |
|---|---|---|
| Profit by Payment Method | Column | Cash dominates at $9M |
| Revenue by Product Line | Horizontal Bar | Healthcare leads at $21.86M |
| Transactions by Region | Area | Africa leads at 818 (40.9%) |
| Revenue/Expense/Profit by Category | Multi-line | R&D peaks across all metrics |
| Average Expense per Category | Pie | Equal distribution 16-17% each |

---

## Key Findings

**Finding 1 — Healthcare dominates revenue**
$21,863,582 — 40.5% of all revenue 
from one product line alone.

**Finding 2 — Africa leads transaction volume**
818 out of 2,000 transactions — more 
than North America and Europe combined.

**Finding 3 — Cash is the top payment method**
$9,013,666 in cash profit — more than 
credit card, bank transfer and PayPal 
combined.

**Finding 4 — R&D drives peak performance**
Highest revenue, expenses and profit 
simultaneously across all categories.

**Finding 5 — Perfectly balanced department expenses**
All 6 departments average 16-17% of 
expenses each — no single department 
dominates cost structure.
